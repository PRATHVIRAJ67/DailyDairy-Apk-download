DailyDairy for Android

Fresh milk and curd at your door every morning, from the dairy that serves your area.

  1. Tap Download and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open DailyDairy, sign in with your mobile number and add your address. Your PIN code shows which dairy delivers to you.

Your phone may warn that this type of file can be harmful. That message appears for every app installed outside the Play Store. The app is from DailyDairy. iPhone: a direct iPhone download isn't available yet.
